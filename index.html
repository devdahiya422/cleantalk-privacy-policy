CleanTalk Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

About CleanTalk
CleanTalk is a Discord moderation bot designed to help Discord server administrators detect and manage profanity, abusive messages, spam, and other unwanted content.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how CleanTalk may handle information when the bot is installed or used on a Discord server.

By adding or using CleanTalk on a Discord server, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

2. Information CleanTalk May Process

CleanTalk is designed to process only information that is necessary for its moderation features.

This may include:

  • Discord user IDs.
  • Discord server IDs.
  • Discord channel IDs.
  • Messages that are processed for moderation and spam detection.
  • Moderation-related configuration set by server administrators.
  • Information provided through Discord interactions or commands.

CleanTalk does not intentionally request or collect passwords, payment card information, or Discord account authentication tokens.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by CleanTalk may be used to:

  • Detect profanity, abusive content, and spam.
  • Automatically remove messages that violate configured rules.
  • Apply moderation settings configured by server administrators.
  • Provide moderation-related responses or notifications.
  • Maintain and improve the reliability of CleanTalk.
  • Prevent misuse of the bot and its moderation features.

4. Message Processing

CleanTalk may inspect messages that are available to the bot in Discord in order to perform its moderation functions.

Depending on the bot's configuration and implementation, a message may be analyzed for prohibited words, abusive variations, spam, or other configured moderation conditions.

CleanTalk is intended to use message content for moderation purposes rather than for advertising or selling personal information.

5. Message Deletion

When CleanTalk detects content that violates the configured moderation rules, it may automatically delete that message if the bot has the required Discord permissions.

Server administrators are responsible for configuring the moderation rules used by their server.

6. Direct Messages and Notifications

Depending on the server's configuration, CleanTalk may provide moderation-related information to a Discord member through a Discord direct message or through the server.

The exact behavior may depend on the settings selected by the server administrator.

7. Data Storage and Retention

CleanTalk aims to minimize the amount of information retained. Information that is not required for the operation of the bot should not be retained unnecessarily.

Where information is stored for configuration, moderation, security, or technical purposes, it may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Actual storage and retention practices may change as CleanTalk develops and new features are introduced.

8. Data Sharing

CleanTalk does not sell personal information.

Information processed by CleanTalk may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to operate the service, comply with applicable legal requirements, protect the service from abuse, or protect the rights and safety of users and the service.

9. Discord

CleanTalk operates through Discord's platform and uses Discord's services and APIs.

Discord may independently collect and process information according to its own policies and terms. CleanTalk does not control Discord's independent data practices.

For more information, please review Discord's Privacy Policy .

10. Security

Reasonable measures are intended to be used to protect information processed by CleanTalk against unauthorized access, misuse, or unnecessary disclosure.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's Privacy

CleanTalk is a Discord application and does not intentionally request personal information directly from children.

Users should use Discord and CleanTalk in accordance with Discord's applicable age requirements and terms.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as CleanTalk develops new features, changes its functionality, or updates its data practices.

When significant changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.

13. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or CleanTalk's privacy practices, you can contact the CleanTalk developer through the official CleanTalk support channels.
